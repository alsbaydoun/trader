# Chart Indicators and AI Reading

Each chart has its own **Indicators & AI reading** section below the price chart. The selected
overlay indicators are drawn directly over the candles; momentum and volatility indicators are
shown as readings with their current values and interpretation.

## Available Indicators

- Overlays: EMA, SMA, Bollinger Bands, VWAP, and Ichimoku Cloud.
- Momentum: RSI, MACD, and Stochastic.
- Trend and volatility: ADX + DI and ATR.
- Structure: recent highs, lows, breakout, and candle direction.

## Use It

1. Open the indicator section under the chart you want to study.
2. Choose an indicator from **Add indicator**, then press the plus button.
3. Use the settings button on a reading to change its period or parameters.
4. Remove an indicator with the x button on its chip.
5. Use a preset when you want a ready-made trend, momentum, or volatility set.

Selections are saved separately for each symbol in the browser on this workstation. Changing the
symbol or timeframe keeps the indicators available for that chart and recalculates them from the
latest candle history.

## AI-Assisted Reading

The reading combines the selected indicator outputs into a transparent **BUY BIAS**, **SELL BIAS**,
or **WAIT** summary. It shows an agreement percentage, the strongest indicator reasons, and a
watch item such as stretched momentum, weak trend strength, spread, or nearby support and
resistance. Agreement is a description of technical alignment, not a probability of profit and
not an order instruction.

The calculation is local and deterministic. It does not claim to be a trained model or replace
the existing signal engine, paper trading, backtesting, news analysis, or risk controls. Confirm
signals on closed candles, include spread and execution costs in testing, and keep the application
in paper mode while evaluating a new indicator combination.
